Reception

Lost and found

Items found anywhere in the urbanisation (the pools, the gardens, the beach, the gym, the courts or the car parks) are held at reception.

If you find something

Hand it in at reception rather than leaving it where it was found. Staff record the item, the place and the date, so it can be matched against enquiries.

If you have lost something

Ask at reception or write to admin@alcazababeach.com with a description, and where and when you last had it. Items can also be claimed on behalf of a guest or a tenant.

How long items are kept

Unclaimed items are held for [30] days and then given to charity or disposed of. Perishable items are not kept.

Documents, keys, cards and valuables. Passports, identity documents, bank cards, telephones, jewellery and cash are not held at reception beyond the day they are found: they are handed to the Policía Local in Estepona, where the owner can recover them on proof of identity. Reception will tell you the date and time of the handover. The community does not accept responsibility for personal property lost in the common areas.
